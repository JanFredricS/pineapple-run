Listening checklist
  • Nothing plays before the first tap/click/key; after it the status shows running.
  • Each theme is recognisably different (beach marimba + shaker, kitchen bouncy squares + woodblocks, workbench muted saw riff + clank, endless steel drum, title ukulele) and never harsh.
  • Stages 0→3 add groove, melody, sparkle; switching is a smooth 2 s cross-fade in time with the beat; rapid clicking never clicks or jumps.
  • The 8-bar loop repeats with no gap or hiccup at the seam (listen across ~20 s).
  • Switching theme cross-fades; Stop fades out.
  • Bounce volume follows the speed slider and is silent below ~0.6 m/s; the storm plays only a few bounces.
  • Motor pitch follows the speed slider smoothly; stop fades without a click. Blender whir starts/stops cleanly.
  • Switch tabs: sound suspends; come back: it resumes. Mute + volumes survive a reload.
  • Destroy: all sound stops immediately; recreate works after another tap.

# Coconut Run reference images

The images are from **Coconut Run** (Johnson Controls "Ingenuity Welcome" recruitment campaign, launched in 2008). The game was built by Fallon and Colossal Squid Industries. The player builds a cart from bars, wheels, and dampers, and the cart carries 15 coconuts over rough terrain to a shredder. The shredded coconut fiber is used to make car seats.

Score = time x coconuts delivered (out of 15), shown as an "Efficiency Rating" percentage.

The art style is blueprint graph paper inside a curled-paper frame. The cart parts are pale blue-grey, the terrain is beige or grey rock with steel girders and pipes, and the site chrome is yellow and orange.

All files were checked with `file` and are valid images.

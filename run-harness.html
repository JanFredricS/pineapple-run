    Keys & acceptance checklist

    ←/→ or A/D drive (or hold the on-screen buttons). Enter: Start, then Release. G: Give Up. R: Reset. Add ?mode=endless to the URL for the endless end rule.

    • Before Start nothing moves; Start logs started and the cart settles under the funnel.
    • Pineapples rest in the V funnel on the plug until Release.
    • Release logs released, removes the plug, the load drops into the cart and the clock starts at 0.
    • Driving works with ←/→, A/D and touch buttons; switching tab / clicking outside the window / a cancelled touch stops the drive.
    • Camera follows the right-most cart body smoothly.
    • A pineapple left on the ground logs pineappleLost about 3 s later; remaining counts down once per pineapple.
    • A pineapple reaching the blender pit's base logs goalReached with the delivered count (pineapples past the line, lost ones included); nothing is logged after it. Cart parts bounce off the blender.
    • Endless mode: losing the last pineapple logs allLost and ends the run.
    • Give Up logs gaveUp with the sim time; the cart stops driving.
    • Loading a broken cart or level JSON (including a schema-valid cart that cannot start) shows an error and keeps the current run.
